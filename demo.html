Counter and table to kitchen

  1. Staff take an orderIn the staff app, pick a table, tap items, then Send to kitchen.
  2. A guest orders by QROn the Table 5 page, pick drinks and food and place the order.
  3. Staff approve the guest orderIn Orders, accept it under Needs approval.
  4. Kitchen makes and servesIn Kitchen: Start, Mark ready, Served. The guest's screen follows along.
